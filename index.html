CASE 01 STATUS: SCORED

Credit Risk Classification

Problem: Predict loan default risk from applicant financial data, where a model's blind spots have real repayment consequences.

Engineered domain-specific features — loan-to-income ratio, interest burden — before touching a model. Benchmarked Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost, selecting the final classifier on cross-validated F1 to guard against overfitting rather than headline accuracy.

Finding: Feature engineering moved the needle more than model choice — a result worth showing lenders who default to "just add more models."

CASE 02 STATUS: PATTERN FOUND

New York Airbnb Listings, 2024 (EDA)

Problem: Surface pricing and demand patterns across 20,000+ NYC listings for a host or platform deciding how to price.

Cleaned raw listing data — missing values, type corrections — across boroughs, room types, and review volume.

Finding: Review count correlates positively with price, meaning reputation itself carries measurable pricing power, independent of location or room type.
